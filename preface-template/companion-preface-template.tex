\begin{itemize}

\item Please provide the preface for your event below.

\item \textbf{MANDATORY}: Use a single \texttt{section} for this (you can include subsections if needed).
The title of your preface should follow the format indicated in Section \ref{sec:preface}.

\item \textbf{MANDATORY}: Your preface should include an \texttt{itemize} list of accepted papers with their authors. Previous prefaces also included the same kind of lists with organizers and reviewers / program committee.

\item You don't need to delete this instructions. We will simply pick the section from your \texttt{.tex} file.

\item No images/figures were needed in previous MODELS Companion prefaces. Please avoid them to simplify frontmatter management.

\item This style is not final. We will combine the prefaces into a single proceedings frontmatter document later.
\end{itemize}
